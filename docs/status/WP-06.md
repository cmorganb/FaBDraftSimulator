# WP-06: Card model, indexes, legality

**Status:** DONE
**Date:** 2026-09-14

## What was built

- `packages/core/fabdraft_core/cards/legality.py` (pure, no I/O):
  - `card_playable_by(card, hero, format_rules=None) -> bool` - the plan
    section 6.3 subset rule, with real citations (see below), where `hero`
    is a `Card` with `object_type == "hero"` (per WP-05's finding that
    heroes are just another row in the same card table, matching the plan
    glossary's "Arena card: Hero, weapon, or equipment"). Raises
    `ValueError` if `hero.object_type != "hero"` - a caller bug, not a
    legality question.
  - `moniker_of(name) -> str` - CR 2.7.3's "moniker" (e.g. "Viserai" for
    both "Viserai, Between Worlds" and "Viserai, the Forsaken"), needed
    because Specialization (CR 8.3.7) matches the hero's moniker, not their
    full name. Verified directly against real ingested IAR data: "Arknight
    Shard" (Viserai Specialization) is legal for both real Viserai hero
    forms and illegal for Levia.
  - `FormatRules` (frozen dataclass, `banned_in_limited: frozenset[str]`) -
    the minimal slice of `*.format.json` (plan section 5.3) legality needs;
    not a full contract, since format-config loading is WP-09's job.
- **Real rule citations**, sourced directly from
  `docs/sources/en-fab-{cr,trp}.txt` (full Comprehensive Rules / Tournament
  Rules text the user added locally - not committed, kept for agents to
  reference; see memory `rules-text-sources`), not guessed or left as
  placeholders:
  - `# TRP 8.2`: "A Player's booster draft card-pool comprises 1 young hero
    card and all cards in their limited-pool, subject to the class and/or
    talents of their hero, keywords, and format-specific restrictions."
  - `# CR 1.1.3`: "A card can only be included in a player's card-pool if
    the card's supertypes are a subset of their hero's supertypes."
  - `# CR 2.11.6` / `2.11.6a` / `2.11.6b`: supertypes are class-or-talent;
    the exact 17 class keywords and 12 talent keywords.
  - `# CR 2.14.1a`: "Generic" in a type box means no supertypes (why an
    empty `classes`/`talents` list is always a legal subset).
  - `# CR 8.3.7` + `# CR 2.7.3`: Specialization and moniker.
- `packages/core/tests/data/legality_cases.json` - hand-checked cases for
  all three real IAR draft heroes (Levia/Brute, Malice/Necromancer,
  "Viserai, Between Worlds"/Runeblade, all Shadow - plan S10, confirmed
  against real data), covering Generic, class-only, talent-only,
  exact-match, mismatched-talent (including the plan's own "Chaos Brute"
  example), mismatched-class, Specialization-match, Specialization-mismatch,
  and one format-ban case. Hero names/classes/talents are the real grounded
  facts; card names in the cases are fictional (never real card text).
- **Fixed a real data-quality bug found while sourcing citations**:
  `scripts/ingest_set.py`'s `KNOWN_CLASSES`/`KNOWN_TALENTS` (used by the
  the-fab-cube ingestion path) were previously "best-effort general FaB
  knowledge" - checking them against CR 2.11.6a/b found they were wrong:
  missing the class "Thief" and the talents "Revered"/"Reviled", and
  included a fabricated talent "Xenan" that isn't real. Both sets now match
  the CR exactly.

## Interfaces exposed

- `fabdraft_core.cards.legality.{card_playable_by, moniker_of, FormatRules}`
- `packages/core/tests/data/legality_cases.json` - reusable by WP-09's deck
  validator tests.

## Decisions taken

- Scoped `card_playable_by` to exactly what its own acceptance criteria
  asks for (class/talent/Specialization/banned-list legality) and left out
  plan section 6.3's incomplete/truncated first pseudocode line about
  `basic_pool_unlimited` (the pseudocode itself trails off with "..." and
  the WP's acceptance criteria doesn't mention Basic-rarity cases at all).
  Pool-membership questions ("was this card drafted, or is it a
  freely-available Basic card") are a *different* question from "would this
  hero's card-pool restrictions allow it" and belong to WP-09's deck
  validator (`NOT_IN_POOL` violation code), not here.
- `Essence`-style meta-static abilities (CR: "you may have [SUPERTYPES]
  cards in your deck as though your hero had those supertypes") are a known
  gap - `card_playable_by` doesn't grant any such exception. Not needed for
  IAR's three draft heroes' base kits; flagged for WP-09 if a drafted card
  ever grants one.

## VERIFY items resolved or escalated

None newly opened. Confirmed real data continues to match plan S10 (three
Shadow-talent draft heroes: Brute, Necromancer, Runeblade).

## Known limitations

- `moniker_of` only strips a trailing comma-suffix; it doesn't strip a
  leading honorific (CR's own "Ser Boltyn" -> "Boltyn" example). No IAR
  hero name uses one, so this is untested against that case - documented
  in the function's own docstring, not silently assumed correct in general.
- `card_playable_by` doesn't yet distinguish "legal to include in the
  card-pool" from "legal to register a full deck" (deck size, arena-slot
  conflicts, etc.) - that's WP-09.

## What the next WP needs to know

- WP-09 (deck build + validation) should call `card_playable_by` per
  candidate card as one of several checks (`ILLEGAL_CARD` violation code),
  layering pool-membership (`NOT_IN_POOL`) and Basic-rarity-unlimited logic
  on top - not inside this function.
- `FormatRules.banned_in_limited` should be populated from
  `format_config.json`'s `format_restrictions` (plan L7, still empty/unused
  in IAR as of this WP) once that gets a real value.
